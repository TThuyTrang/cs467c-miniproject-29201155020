CS467C: Synthetic recommendation evaluation
Split seed: 467; train ratings: 180; test ratings: 60
Protocol: four randomly held-out ratings per user; no selection by rating value.
Relevance: held-out rating >= 4; rank all catalogue activities not seen in training.
Precision@5 = held-out relevant hits / 5, averaged over all 15 users.
Unjudged items count as no hit, not as known dislikes; fewer than five results are penalized.
Users with no relevant test items: 1 (included with zero hits).
Maximum measurable mean Precision@5 with this holdout: 0.333333.
TF-IDF uses catalogue metadata; all profiles, neighbors, means and sentiment use train only.

popularity        0.040000     3     15               75

Limitations: 15 synthetic users, templated rating-conditioned journals, sparse judgments, one split, and no evidence of effectiveness on real users. Weights were fixed before evaluation; no test-set tuning or significance claim is made.
